# MBTI axes → communication implications

Use these as **tendencies** to mix together, not rigid rules. A person's observed behavior and
stated prefs override anything here. Most people are a blend; lean on the axes the type expresses
most strongly.

## E ↔ I  (Extraversion / Introversion) — *where they process*
| | Extraversion (E) | Introversion (I) |
|---|---|---|
| Prefers | Talking it out live, brainstorming aloud, quick back-and-forth | Time to think first, written/async, reflect before replying |
| Give them | A call/huddle, room to riff, prompt responses | Agenda ahead of time, a doc to read, space to respond later |
| Avoid | Long silences, "just email me" for everything | Cold-calling for an on-the-spot decision, forcing fast verbal answers |

## S ↔ N  (Sensing / Intuition) — *what info they trust*
| | Sensing (S) | Intuition (N) |
|---|---|---|
| Prefers | Concrete facts, specifics, step-by-step, proven examples | Big picture, the "why", patterns, possibilities, vision |
| Give them | Clear details, data, exactly what/when/how | The concept first, then let them connect dots; headline > minutiae |
| Avoid | Vague abstractions, "we'll figure out details later" | Drowning them in detail, burying the point under process |

## T ↔ F  (Thinking / Feeling) — *how they decide*
| | Thinking (T) | Feeling (F) |
|---|---|---|
| Convinced by | Logic, competence, cost/benefit, fairness, efficiency | People impact, values, harmony, sincerity, who's affected |
| Give them | The rationale, trade-offs, straight talk | Warmth first, acknowledge feelings, frame around the team |
| Avoid | Over-padding, emotional appeals with no substance | Blunt critique with no warmth, treating people as variables |

## J ↔ P  (Judging / Perceiving) — *how they organize*
| | Judging (J) | Perceiving (P) |
|---|---|---|
| Prefers | Plans, decisions, clear deadlines, closure, no surprises | Options open, flexibility, exploring before committing |
| Give them | A clear ask + deadline, structure, advance notice of changes | Choices not ultimatums, room to adapt, "here are a few paths" |
| Avoid | Last-minute changes, ambiguity, reopening settled decisions | Premature lock-in, rigid "decide now", over-scheduling |

## Quick persuasion-lever map
- **NT** (e.g. INTJ, ENTP): vision + logic + competence; respect their autonomy.
- **NF** (e.g. INFP, ENFJ): meaning + people + authenticity; lead with the why and the human stakes.
- **ST** (e.g. ISTJ, ESTP): facts + results + efficiency; be concrete and reliable.
- **SF** (e.g. ISFJ, ESFP): practical help + relationships + warmth; specifics with a personal touch.

## Style clashes (common friction → bridge)
- **T sender ↔ F recipient:** add a warm opener + acknowledge impact before the logic.
- **N sender ↔ S recipient:** translate the vision into concrete next steps and examples.
- **J sender ↔ P recipient:** offer options and a "we can adjust" instead of a hard mandate.
- **E sender ↔ I recipient:** send context in writing first; don't demand an instant verbal answer.
